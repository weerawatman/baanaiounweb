# Sales & Persuasion

สรุปย่อ: การขายที่ดีคือการวินิจฉัย ไม่ใช่การโน้มน้าว — คนไม่ซื้อสินค้า แต่ซื้อการเปลี่ยนสถานะของตัวเอง

## Discovery before pitch

- Diagnose pain, budget, timeline, and decision-maker before presenting anything. A pitch without discovery is spam.
- People buy outcomes, stated in their words: not "รีโนเวทครบวงจร" but "ย้ายเข้าได้ใน 90 วัน ไม่ต้องคุมช่างเอง".
- The best qualification question is about consequence: "ถ้ายังหาบ้านไม่ได้อีก 6 เดือน กระทบอะไรบ้าง?"

## Copy that converts (web)

- One page, one job. Every section either advances the visitor toward the CTA or gets cut.
- Above the fold: who it's for + the outcome + one action. Clarity beats cleverness every time.
- Specificity is credibility: "ปิดดีลแล้ว 47 หลังในบ้านบึง" > "ประสบการณ์สูง". Numbers, names, photos.
- Address the top objection *on the page* (price? trust? process?) — unspoken objections don't disappear, they bounce.
- CTA = verb + outcome, low commitment first: "รับการประเมินฟรี" > "Submit".

## Trust architecture (high-trust purchases like real estate)

Trust stacks in order: proof of results (before/after) → proof of people (reviews, faces, real names) → proof of process (steps, timelines) → reversal of risk (free assessment, no-obligation). Lead with the strongest layer you honestly have.

## Objections & follow-up

- Objection = interest + missing information. Isolate it ("ถ้าเรื่องราคาโอเค มีเรื่องอื่นอีกไหม?") before answering it.
- Speed-to-lead dominates: reply in minutes, not days — conversion decays hourly.
- Follow-up adds value each touch (new listing, market info), never "just checking in".

## Ethics line

Persuade toward a decision the customer would still endorse a year later. Scarcity/urgency only when real. Long-term referral business dies on one manipulated deal.

## Cross-discipline links

- With **frontend-craft**: conversion copy and visual hierarchy are the same discipline — the eye path IS the pitch.
- With **data-analysis**: every promise on the page should map to a funnel step you measure.
- With **strategy-consulting**: sales feedback is the cheapest market research; route lost-deal reasons back to positioning monthly.
