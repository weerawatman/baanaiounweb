# Strategy

สรุปย่อ: กลยุทธ์คือการเลือก — เลือกว่าจะชนะที่ไหน ด้วยอะไร และตั้งใจ*ไม่ทำ*อะไร

## Structured problem solving

1. **Define the question sharply.** "โตยังไง?" is not a question; "เพิ่ม lead คุณภาพจากบ้านบึงภายใน 6 เดือนด้วยงบเท่าเดิมยังไง?" is.
2. **Decompose MECE** (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive): growth = traffic × conversion × deal size × close rate. Fix the weakest multiplier first.
3. **Hypothesis first, analysis second.** State your best answer on day one, then design the minimum analysis that could kill it. Analysis without a hypothesis is tourism.
4. **80/20 relentlessly.** The first 20% of analysis changes the decision; the rest changes the slides.

## Strategic choices

- Strategy = where to play + how to win. If your plan works equally well for every competitor, it is not a strategy.
- Positioning: pick the customer for whom you are the *obvious* choice, accept being wrong for everyone else. (บ้านไออุ่น: investor-led, renovation-backed, bilingual care in Ban Bueng/EEC — not "everything for everyone in Thailand".)
- Moats compound: local trust, repeat referrals, and a co-agent network grow with use; paid traffic does not.
- Sequence beats scope: nail one geography/segment, then replicate the playbook.

## Market sizing (back-of-envelope)

TAM → SAM → SOM with stated assumptions. Triangulate top-down (population × rates) against bottom-up (capacity × conversion); if they disagree 10×, an assumption is broken — that's the insight.

## Recommendation format

Situation → complication → answer → 3 supporting reasons → risks and what would change our mind → first next step with owner and date.

## Cross-discipline links

- With **sales-persuasion**: strategy chooses the battlefield; sales wins the battles. If sales keeps losing, either execution or the battlefield choice is wrong — check both.
- With **data-analysis**: strategy sets the hypothesis; data sizes and tests it.
- With **creative-direction**: differentiation must be *felt* in the product's look and voice, or it only exists in the deck.
