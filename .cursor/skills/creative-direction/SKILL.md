# Creative Direction

สรุปย่อ: ศิลปะในงานสร้างคือการเลือกอย่างมีเจตนา — ทุกอย่างที่อยู่ในเฟรมต้องมีเหตุผลว่าทำไมถึงอยู่ และทำไมถึงอยู่*แบบนั้น*

## Concept before craft

- Every strong piece has one idea you can say in a sentence. บ้านไออุ่น = "ความอบอุ่นของบ้าน ส่งผ่านคนที่เคยเหนื่อยกับการหาบ้านมาก่อน". Every color, photo, and word either serves that or dilutes it.
- If you can't articulate why a design feels right, you can't repeat it — and can't defend it. Build the vocabulary: contrast, rhythm, tension, negative space, hierarchy.
- Mood boards before mockups: collect 10 references, articulate what each one does, steal the *principle*, never the surface.

## Composition (visual or verbal)

- The eye enters, travels, and rests — design that journey. One focal point; everything else supports.
- Negative space is not empty; it's what makes the subject audible. When a layout feels cluttered, remove before rearranging.
- Contrast creates meaning: big/small, warm/cool, dense/sparse. No contrast, no message.
- Repetition builds rhythm and brand; variation at the right moment creates emphasis. (Same card style ×6, one highlighted = the highlight speaks.)

## Storytelling

- Structure: someone we care about → wants something → faces obstacles → transforms. The Baan Ai Oun timeline (2002 → 2026) works because it's this arc, not a corporate history.
- Specific detail beats abstract virtue: "ห้องเช่าราคาหลักพัน ย้ายบ่อย" lands; "เราเข้าใจลูกค้า" evaporates.
- Voice = consistent choices in vocabulary, sentence length, warmth. Write down 5 words the brand would use and 5 it never would.

## Critique method (giving or receiving)

1. What is this trying to do? (intent)
2. Is it doing it? (effect — separate from taste)
3. What's the strongest element? Protect it.
4. What competes with the strongest element? Cut or quiet it.
Never "I don't like it"; always "this element pulls attention from X".

## Taste is trained

Consume above your level daily; copy masters by hand to feel the decisions; ship, get response, adjust. Taste is the residue of a thousand comparisons — nobody is born with it.

## Cross-discipline links

- With **frontend-craft**: craft executes; direction decides. A pixel-perfect page with no concept is wallpaper.
- With **sales-persuasion**: emotion opens the door, logic justifies the walk-through — art is the door.
- With **scientific-thinking**: A/B tests can rank two ideas; only taste can generate the third one worth testing.
